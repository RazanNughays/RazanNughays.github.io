Research Interests

My research examines how nanoscale structure and interfaces influence ultrafast energy transport in materials relevant to renewable energy and optoelectronic technologies.

Ultrafast Optical Probes

I develop and apply time-resolved nonlinear optical techniques to investigate charge transfer, exciton dynamics, and symmetry-dependent processes at surfaces and heterointerfaces.

Electron-Based Probes

Using ultrafast electron microscopy, I study carrier transport and recombination with direct spatial resolution, enabling visualization of dynamic processes that are inaccessible to purely optical methods.

Interface-Driven Phenomena

A central theme of my work is understanding how structural heterogeneity, symmetry breaking, and electronic coupling at interfaces dictate material performance.

Instrumentation Development

I am particularly interested in building experimental platforms that combine spatial precision with femtosecond temporal resolution to directly correlate nanoscale structure with dynamic function.
